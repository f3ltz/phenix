Finding 1: Tree Ensembles Dominate Interpolation

Augmenting features with 32 Phylo-PCoA spatial eigenmaps lifts explained variance from R² ≈ 0.00 to 0.849 (Random Forest). Non-phylogenetic climate and genomic baselines fail because distant species share overlapping bioclimates.

Mass Factor Precision: ±2.1× across 8 orders of magnitude
Finding 2: XGBoost Excels in Out-of-Clade Extrapolation

Under rigorous Phylogenetic Cross-Validation (Phylo-CV with 140 Ma buffer zones), XGBoost achieves the lowest test RMSE (1.274). Gradient boosting with depth constraints anchors predictions and resists ancestral drift better than unconstrained trees.

Extrapolation Resilience: Lowest error on held-out mammalian orders
